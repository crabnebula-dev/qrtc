Vendored crates

qrtc builds on str0m, a sans-I/O WebRTC stack, and on dimpl, its DTLS library. Both are vendored under vendor/ with small patches, each listed in the crate's PATCHES.md and meant for upstream.

Why vendor

  • Post-quantum DTLS needs changes inside dimpl's handshake, which a wrapper cannot reach.
  • Interoperability with LiveKit and perfect negotiation needs two behaviour changes in str0m.
  • Keeping the build free of C means replacing the one path that pulled in AWS-LC.
  • Path dependencies apply the patches to every project that depends on qrtc, with no [patch] section on their side.

Only sources, manifests, licences and readmes are vendored. Upstream tests, examples and dev-dependencies are dropped, so the SBOM lists only what is built.

dimpl 0.7.4

The patches add post-quantum groups to DTLS 1.3 and change nothing for classical groups. Every changed spot is marked in the source.

#ChangeFiles
1Named groups: MLKEM512, MLKEM768, MLKEM1024, SECP256R1MLKEM768, X25519MLKEM768, and X25519MLKEM512_PRIVATE (0xfe5c). The supported list grows from 4 to 10, and group lists use that capacity, so a peer offering many groups is no longer rejected.src/types.rs
2SupportedKxGroup::server_exchange takes the client's share and returns the server's share and the secret. Its default runs the Diffie-Hellman steps.src/crypto/provider.rs, src/dtls13/server.rs
3SupportedKxGroup::dtls12() keeps KEM groups out of DTLS 1.2 offers, selection and key exchangesrc/dtls12/
4The provider self-test exchanges keys as a TLS 1.3 client and server, covering both kinds of groupsrc/crypto/validation/mod.rs
5A ClientHello with a hybrid share (about 1.4 kB) is split into MTU-sized records, per RFC 9147 section 5.5. The forked 1.2 or 1.3 handshake learns how many epoch-0 records it used.src/auto.rs, src/dtls12/client.rs, src/dtls13/client.rs, src/dtls13/engine.rs
6A ServerHello split over several records is DTLS 1.3, since a DTLS 1.2 ServerHello always fits one. The 1.3 client then reassembles it.src/auto.rs

The patched crate passes its own unit tests: 251 with rust-crypto and 301 with the default features, run from a copy with the upstream dev-dependencies restored.
